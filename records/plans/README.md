# records/plans/

Archived implementation plans, produced by the Research → Plan → Annotate → Implement pipeline
(the `research-plan-implement` skill).

**This is the archive, not the working directory.** A plan lands here when its work is finished
or abandoned — while it is being executed it lives wherever the pipeline puts it, in the repo
where the work is happening.

## What to archive

- The plan as it was actually executed, including the human annotations. The annotations are the
  most valuable part: they are where a person corrected the machine, and they are what makes the
  next plan better.
- Plans that were abandoned. Say why. An abandoned plan explains a road not taken, which is
  otherwise invisible six months later.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-kebab-title.md`, dated to when the work concluded.

Add a status line under the title: `**Status:** implemented | abandoned | superseded by <path>`,
and a line naming the sibling repo and commit range the plan produced, if any.
